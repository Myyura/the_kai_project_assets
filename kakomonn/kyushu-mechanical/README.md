# Kyushu University mechanical engineering exam figures

Figures accompanying the 2010–2019 admission-year questions collected by The Kai Project.

- 114 original question figure crops, taken from archived official Kyushu University papers. The original figures are attributed to Kyushu University.
- 29 original solution diagrams, created by 祭音Myyura with GPT 6 Astra refinement.

`sources.json` records the official archive URLs, PDF page/crop coordinates where applicable, and SHA-256 hashes. Original question crops preserve the source pixels; separate crops may be arranged together for readability. Solution diagrams are distinguished from question figures.
