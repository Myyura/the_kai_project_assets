# University of Tokyo mechanical engineering entrance examination, 2027 admission

These 25 PNG scans were supplied by [cdc95-04 in The Kai Project PR #151](https://github.com/Myyura/the_kai_project/pull/151). They are archived byte for byte; `source-manifest.json` records their original SHA-256 digests and byte counts. The examination statements and figures belong to the University of Tokyo.

The Japanese covers identify the examination date as 1 September 2026: Part I, 13:00–14:00; Part II, 15:00–16:30. There are 13 Japanese pages including both covers and 12 English pages. Files containing `_en_p` are English pages. The other numbered pages are Japanese. Part I problem 1 contains sections I and II; its second English page contains the end of section I and the beginning of section II. Handwritten marks are part of the supplied scans, not examination text.

The university's [admissions and past-examinations page](https://www2.mech.t.u-tokyo.ac.jp/admissions/?lang=ja) was checked on 17 October 2026 and did not list the 2027 examination papers. These files therefore cite the contributor's scans as the direct source; they are not represented as downloads from a university-hosted paper or as official solutions.

Necessary figure crops are stored one directory above this source archive and referenced beside the transcribed questions in The Kai Project.
